# NextSkip Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 17, 2026

NextSkip is a browser extension that automatically skips Netflix recap and intro segments according to your settings.

## Information the extension handles

- **Netflix page content and episode URL:** While you are on `www.netflix.com`, NextSkip checks the player interface for “Skip Intro” and “Skip Recap” controls and reads the current episode identifier from the page URL to resume on a new episode. This processing happens locally in your browser. The extension does not save this page content or URL.
- **Your preferences:** NextSkip saves your intro and recap toggle settings using Chrome's Storage Sync API. Chrome may sync these preferences across your signed-in browsers according to your Chrome Sync settings.

## How information is used and shared

The page content and episode URL are used only to detect and skip the Netflix controls enabled in the extension. Your preferences are used to apply your choices. NextSkip does not send Netflix page content, episode URLs, or preferences to the developer or to analytics, advertising, or other third-party services. Chrome Sync may store and synchronize the preferences as part of the Chrome service.

The extension does not sell user information, use it for advertising, or allow the developer to access it.

## Retention and control

Page content and episode URLs are processed temporarily in the browser and are not retained by NextSkip. You can change or remove saved preferences in the extension popup or by removing the extension. Chrome Sync data is subject to your Chrome account and sync settings.
